What We Do

Championing Artistry, Discipline & Mentorship

World-Class Competition

The Washington International Rachmaninoff Music Competition (WIRMC) brings together outstanding young performers from across the globe on an international stage.

Music Education

We provide masterclasses, workshops, and educational resources to help musicians develop their artistry and reach their full potential.

Global Connections

Building bridges across cultures through the universal language of music, connecting talented individuals with mentors and opportunities worldwide.
